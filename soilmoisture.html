Understanding Soil Moisture for Agriculture

Soil moisture is crucial for healthy crops, with levels ranging from 0 (very dry) to 1 (fully saturated). Here's a guide to understanding moisture levels and the actions you can take.

Soil Moisture Levels

  • 0.0 - 0.1: Very Dry
  • 0.1 - 0.2: Low Moisture
  • 0.2 - 0.3: Just Right
  • 0.3 - 0.4: Too Much Water
  • 0.4 and above: Waterlogged

Actions Based on Soil Moisture Levels

0.0 - 0.1: Very Dry

Water crops immediately; check your irrigation system; add mulch to retain moisture.

0.1 - 0.2: Low Moisture

Water if rain isn't expected; regularly check soil moisture; adjust watering based on crop needs.

0.2 - 0.3: Just Right

No additional watering needed; continue to monitor moisture levels regularly.

0.3 - 0.4: Too Much Water

Reduce watering to prevent waterlogging; improve drainage if needed.

0.4 and above: Waterlogged

Drain excess water quickly; avoid planting sensitive crops during wet conditions.
